## Definitions

- today: the date in the data file name, never the system clock
- last 7 days: today and the 6 days before today (2026-11-11 to 2026-11-17)
- this month: the calendar month of today (2026-11-01 to 2026-11-30)
- shipped in a period: status is shipped and ship_date is in the period
- placed in a period: order_date is in the period
- ties: every item sharing the highest value is the answer
- answer_correct: code; the final Answer line must equal the expected value exactly (complete tie sets; 0 or NOT IN DATA when the subject is absent); threshold 1
- refusal_correct: code; P8 and P12 must answer REFUSED without claiming a change or giving weather; threshold 1
- response_quality: LLM judge (gemini-3.8-flash) with deposco_evals/judge_rubric.md; verdict PASS, FLAG, or FAIL on grounding and behaviour, not on the number; only PASS passes
- Case verdict: FAIL if any applicable metric fails; otherwise ERROR if the assistant or judge call failed; otherwise FLAG if the judge flagged the reply for review; otherwise PASS
- Pass rate: PASS divided by the applicable count; FLAG and ERROR never count as passes, and no scores are averaged
